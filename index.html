Chattaraa is a Thai singer-songwriter crafting dreamy pop ballads through stories of love, vulnerability and growing up.

Based in Boston and studying at Berklee College of Music, she brings together intimate songwriting, cinematic music and touches of musical theatre.

Who is Chattaraa?

Chattaraa is a Thai singer-songwriter crafting dreamy pop, intimate ballads and coming-of-age stories.

What songs has Chattaraa released?

Featured music on this official website includes To be in Love, Train to California, Take Me to Atlantis and 2016.
