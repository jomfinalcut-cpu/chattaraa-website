Artist Bio

Chattaraa is a Thai singer-songwriter crafting a world of dreamy pop, intimate ballads, and coming-of-age storytelling. Writing primarily in English, she explores love and growing up through a distinctly feminine lens, capturing the rush, vulnerability, and romanticism of young adulthood.

Based in Boston and studying Music Business/Management at Berklee College of Music, she blends emotional pop songwriting with cinematic production and a theatrical sensibility shaped by growing up immersed in theatre.

Through songs including “Train to California” and “Take Me to Atlantis,” her work bridges her Thai roots with her life in the U.S., creating music that feels deeply personal while resonating across cultures.

Her next chapter, the six-song EP (So Romantic!), explores the moments, emotions, and transformations of falling in love through lush pop production, layered harmonies, and a romantic visual universe.

Working Profile

Singer-songwriter and performing artist. Her work brings songwriting, storytelling, performance, and visual identity together, from intimate Boston showcases to stages in Thailand.

Academic Profile

Music Business/Management at Berklee College of Music, Boston.
